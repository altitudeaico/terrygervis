About

From a Hackney bomb site to Buckingham Palace.

Born in East London to Caribbean parents from Jamaica and Guyana, Terry grew up playing on wartime bomb sites — the rubble of factories destroyed a decade earlier. Where others saw rubble, he saw possibility. That instinct built a forty-year career.
