About

From a Hackney bomb site to Buckingham Palace.

Born in Hackney, East London, to Caribbean parents from Jamaica and Guyana, Terry grew up in post-war Britain. His playgrounds were bomb sites — the rubble of factories destroyed a decade earlier. Where others saw rubble, he saw possibility.

An early fascination with anything mechanical — cars, planes, spaceships, radio — and with collecting comics and books shaped a lifetime of creativity and drive. At fourteen he was trading comic books between London and New York. At sixteen he self-financed his first film, a documentary on Sam Cooke. The BBC recruited him at twenty-one and paid for his training; by twenty-seven he was one of the youngest heads of department in its history.

Forty years on, the through-line is unchanged: take a story that was overlooked, and make it impossible to overlook again.
