Creativity keeps what matters in living memory — and gets people ready for what’s next.

Forty years of work across film, television, publishing, defence and technology, held together by one idea: the stories a country forgets are the ones it most needs to carry forward.

The engineers not born yet

StaRRship H.O.P.E. — a virtual spacecraft built to put space engineering in front of young people.

Why these are one project

A commemorative coin and a virtual spacecraft look like different businesses. They aren’t. Both take something a generation was never told — who flew those aircraft, who gets to build the next ones — and make it real enough to hold, share and act on. Heritage that only looks backwards is nostalgia. This looks both ways.
