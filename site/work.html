Case study · 01

StaRRship H.O.P.E.

A virtual spacecraft — a digital twin of a real-world design developed with Rolls-Royce and inspired by the Royal Air Force. It operates on real-world technology with guidance from defence and civil space experts including RAF Space Command, the UK Space Agency, ESA, USAF Space Force and NASA.

Its job is educational: putting space engineering, and the careers behind it, in front of young people who have never been shown that door.

  • BriefMake the UK’s space economy legible to the next generation
  • RoleCreator; contract with Rolls-Royce SVP David Gordon
  • OutcomeSTEM Women in Defence Award; featured by the London Chamber of Commerce

Case study · 02

Pilots of the Caribbean & Tuskegee Airmen

A commemorative collection honouring the Caribbean and African-American pilots of the Second World War — airmen who fought fascism abroad and racism at home, and whose history was allowed to fade. Two sculpted coins, a piece of 1944 Merlin engine, and archive material from the war years, produced under Royal Warrant.

  • BriefPut a mislaid history into people’s hands, permanently
  • RoleCreative producer, via Legacy & Heritage Ltd
  • OutcomeFeatured in the RAF Club annual magazine; US launch at the Obama Presidential Center, August 2026

Case study · 03

2000 Today — the Millennium broadcast

Jervis Entertainment Media was founded to facilitate a venture with the BBC: broadcasting the year 2000 as it arrived around the planet. A BBC/ABC/WGBH co-production, watched across the world, and still one of the largest coordinated broadcasts ever made.

  • BriefOne night, every timezone, live
  • RoleProduced and directed transmissions
  • OutcomePart of a body of work seen or heard by over 4.5 billion people

Case study · 04

Spirit of the Pharaoh

The first superhero VR game, launched with Richard Branson at Virgin Megastore in 1995 — when VR was a promise, not a product. The original graphic novel and BBC animation were rewritten in 2018 with Marvel and Transformers writer Simon Furman and Superman/Batman writer Stan Berkowitz.

  • BriefAn original superhero universe, playable before its time
  • RoleCreator, producer
  • OutcomeA 30-year IP still in development; animated remake in progress

Case study · 05

The RAF merchandising mandate

The first commercial merchandising agent appointment for the Secretary of State for Defence: building licensing and commercial development for the Royal Air Force brand. It is the working relationship that underpins the coin collection and StaRRship — proof the big names on this site are contracts, not name-drops.

  • BriefMake an institution’s heritage commercially sustainable
  • RoleCommercial merchandising agent — a first for the MOD
  • OutcomeThe foundation for two of the projects on this page
