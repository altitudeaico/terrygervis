THE STORY

Two Air Forces.
One extraordinary legacy.

From the skies over Europe and the Caribbean to the engines that powered victory, this collection tells a shared chapter of World War II history.

Honouring the Royal Air Force and the United States Army Air Forces, it brings together the stories of the Pilots of the Caribbean and the Tuskegee Airmen, alongside the people, aircraft and engineering that shaped their era.

OUR MISSION

Why this collection exists.

Build a reputable collectable medal, coin and memorabilia business, while showcasing British and American excellence in engineering and craftsmanship.

Educate

Bring a new awareness of Black achievements in the context of world history, and correct the failings of institutions in obscuring that history.

Empower

Build a strong economic foundation in the creative industries, placing cultural and financial value in the ownership of intellectual property and physical assets associated with Black culture worldwide.

Promote Leadership

Gift a permanent honour medal representing leadership, freedom and democracy to the Obama Foundation and Presidential Center, as our founder has done in the UK office of state.

A Prosperous Future

Honouring those who suffered from the birth of America and under the British Empire, as a symbol of healing between two nations and a tribute to contributions whose stories mostly go unheard.

We are always in service to our customers. Charity and giving back will always be at the heart of our profit.

THE FOUNDER

Terry Jervis

Innovator, entrepreneur, defence and media executive

With over 40 years of experience in film, television, radio, publishing and digital media, Terry Jervis has always put leadership values at the heart of business. It has taken him from an illustrious BBC and Hollywood media career into heavy industry and defence.

He was brought into the Royal Air Force in 2001 by Sir David Walker, former Equerry to Her Majesty Queen Elizabeth II.

Terry is in living memory and captured the stories told to him by the Pilots of the Caribbean and the Tuskegee Airmen. The brand started with an award-winning exhibition in collaboration with the RAF Museum and Black Cultural Archives, and it continues to grow as that legacy is celebrated.
