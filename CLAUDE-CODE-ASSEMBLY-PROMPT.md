## Build steps — follow this order, verify each with ffprobe before moving on

1. CONFORM every video segment to a common profile FIRST
   (this prevents every xfade failure later):
   scale=1920:1080:force_original_aspect_ratio=decrease,
   pad=1920:1080:(ow-iw)/2:(oh-ih)/2,
   fps=25, settb=AVTB, setsar=1, format=yuv420p
   - heroes.mp4 is 720p/24fps — upscale + retime to 25 (it's 8s, fine)
   - ALWAYS -map 0:v:0 (phone/exported MP4s can carry a thumbnail stream)
   - Strip ALL source audio (-an) — segments are built silent

2. Segment B from the still:
   zoompan slow push (zoom 1.0→1.08 over 8s @25fps), same conform chain.
   Segment D: take the last ~5s of reveal.mp4, setpts=2.0*PTS to
   stretch to 10s (slow hold reads as intentional).
   Segment E: loop endcard.png for 8s, same conform.

3. Join with 0.6s crossfades (xfade), offsets computed as
   (cumulative duration − fade). With all inputs conformed identically
   xfade will configure; if it refuses, the streams differ — re-check
   step 1 rather than fighting the filter. Chain: A xfade B xfade C
   xfade D xfade E → silent picture lock ~87.6s (fades absorb ~2.4s).
   Use transition=fade except C→D: use transition=fadewhite (the
   white-bloom language from the spec).

4. Brand bug: overlay emblem.png bottom-right from 0:00 to end,
   width ~134px (7% of frame), 24px margins, ~85% opacity:
   [emb]format=rgba,colorchannelmixer=aa=0.85[e];
   [v][e]overlay=W-w-24:H-h-24
   This covers the "Generated by AI" watermark region on Reg's clip.
   Check the watermark IS bottom-right on reveal.mp4 first — if it's
   another corner, move the bug there instead.

5. Captions: burn captions.srt with subtitles filter, styled:
   subtitles=captions.srt:force_style='FontName=Cinzel,FontSize=13,
   PrimaryColour=&H0086CEE8,OutlineColour=&H600D0602,BorderStyle=1,
   Outline=1,Shadow=0,MarginV=60,Alignment=2'
   (Colour is gold #E8CE86 in BGR hex. If Cinzel isn't installed:
   fc-list | grep -i cinzel; if absent, download Cinzel from Google
   Fonts into ~/.fonts and fc-cache, or fall back to EB Garamond,
   or Georgia as last resort.)

6. Audio mix:
   - Place VO takes at: s1→1.0s, s3→17s, s4→27s, s5→45s, s6→57s,
     s7→73s, s8→83s (use adelay per take, amix)
   - Music full length, ducked under VO via sidechaincompress
     (threshold 0.05, ratio 8, attack 20, release 400), music at
     -20 LUFS-ish (volume≈0.5 then judge), VO normalised loud and
     clear (loudnorm I=-16 on the VO bus)
   - afade out the music over the last 2s
   - Final loudness pass: loudnorm=I=-14:TP=-1.5 on the mix
     (platform-friendly)

7. Final encode: libx264, -preset veryfast, -crf 19, -pix_fmt yuv420p,
   AAC 192k. The 90s encode may exceed the command timeout — run with
   nohup in the background and poll, don't wait on it.

8. VERIFY (do not skip):
   - ffprobe: duration 87–91s, 1920x1080, 25fps, one video + one audio stream
   - Extract frames at 4s, 20s, 60s, 85s — confirm captions render,
     bug is in place, no black gaps at the joins
   - Extract 3s of audio at 18s — confirm VO is audible over music
   Report what you verified and what you couldn't.
