# Ascent Collection — Hero Film VO Pack
## ElevenLabs generation guide · draft cut · 10 Aug 2026

## The read
Mid-Atlantic gravitas. Measured, warm, museum-documentary — not sales.
Think national-archive narrator, slight British lean. ~140 wpm.

## Settings (ElevenLabs)
- Model: Eleven Multilingual v2 (or v3 if available)
- Stability ~0.50 · Similarity ~0.75 · Style ~0.30
- Speed: 0.95 (a touch slower than default)
- Pick a deep male voice with UK/mid-Atlantic colour. Audition 2–3;
  the test line is Take 1 — if "held in your hands" lands with weight,
  the voice is right.

## Generate as SEVEN separate takes (one per scene; scene 2 is silent).
Separate takes are far easier to place at exact timecodes than one long
read. Name them vo-s1.mp3 … vo-s8.mp3 (no s2).
