# Ascent Collection — Flow / Gemini / Veo Prompt Pack
### Generation prompts for Treatment v2 "The Keeper". Built with the photoreal-prompt method.

**How to use.** Google Flow (Veo) for text-to-video environments and for
image-to-video animation ("frames to video"); Gemini/Imagen ("Nano
Banana") for stills and outpaint. Generate 16:9, highest resolution
offered, 5–10s clips, **no on-clip text/logos** (we add titles in the
edit). Generate 2–3 variants of each and keep the best.

**Two hard rules:**
1. **Real people are supplied, not generated.** Animate the archival
   photos (heroes group, women at war, airman portraits) with image-to-
   video. Never text-to-generate new likenesses of real historical people.
2. **Lock the look.** Append the STYLE SUFFIX below to every environment /
   text-to-video prompt so all clips match.
