### For the general-public / retail version · to pair with Terry's upbeat script

**Date:** 12 Aug 2026
**Why this exists.** Reginald likes the current cut for museums and
institutions (the reverent, slow, orchestral "Ezra" film). For the general
public he wants something **more upbeat** — the phrase from the feedback was
*"a bit punchy but still regal."* This brief is what I'd hand to a composer,
or paste into **Suno / Udio**, or use to pick a **library track** (Artlist,

## The one-line brief
> **Cinematic heritage-pop / orchestral-hybrid.** Regal brass and strings
> meet a modern, driving rhythm section. Proud, warm, uplifting — a legacy
> being celebrated, not mourned. ~95s, builds to a triumphant final lift.

## Feel & references
- **Reference feel** (not to copy): the uplift of a luxury-brand launch film
  or an Olympic "hero" package — orchestra + subtle contemporary pulse.
- **Adjectives to keep:** regal, proud, warm, cinematic, ascendant, elegant.
- **Adjectives to avoid:** somber, funereal, sparse, corporate-generic,
  aggressive, EDM-drop, hip-hop trap, novelty.
- **Keep the "still regal":** real (or convincing) **brass and strings** stay
  the lead voices — the "punch" comes from **rhythm and tempo**, not from
  swapping the orchestra for synths.

## Tempo, key, structure
- **Tempo:** 100–112 BPM (vs the museum cut's ~70 BPM rubato feel).
- **Key/mode:** major or lydian-bright; hopeful, not melancholic.
- **Metre:** 4/4, clear downbeats, light driving percussion (soft taiko / felt
  kick / tambourine shimmer), tasteful — never busy.

**Arc to hit the script beats (~95s):**
| Section | Time | Energy | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Intro | 0–12s | soft, inviting | warm strings + single piano/harp motif; establish the theme |
| Build 1 | 12–36s | rising | add pulse + light percussion under the "Introducing…" / product beats |
| Lift 1 | 36–52s | bright, open | full strings; the aircraft / "celebrating the Spitfire…" montage soars |
| Groove | 52–74s | confident, steady | rhythm forward under the artefacts list — keeps momentum, doesn't sag |
| Build 2 | 74–84s | anticipatory | brass swells under "Only 500…" and the price |
| Triumph | 84–95s | full, uplifting | biggest brass + strings hit on the closing line; resolve warm, not abrupt |

## Instrumentation
Strings (full section), French horns + trumpets for the regal colour, harp or
felt piano for the intimate opening, a restrained modern rhythm bed (soft
percussion, subtle plucked bass), optional light choral "ah" pad on the final
lift. **No vocals with words** (the VO owns the words).
